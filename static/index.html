How the estimate works

A gradient boosting model learned prices from phones listed at two Kampala shops, mobileshop.ug and dondolo.co.ug, using brand, product line, tier, release year, condition and specs. Half of its estimates land within 7% of a shop's real price. For models neither shop sells, expect them to be about 12% off. The shaded range is where 8 in 10 real prices fall.
